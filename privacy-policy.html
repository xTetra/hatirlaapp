Hatırla Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tetra Yazılım ("we," "us," or "our") processes information in the Hatırla Android application (the "App"). Hatırla is designed as an offline-first personal notes, tasks, and reminders application. The contents of your notes, tasks, reminders, PIN, backup password, and audio recordings are not sent to servers operated by us.

1. Information stored locally

The following information is stored in the App's private storage on your device:

  • notes, tasks, reminder text, dates, recurrence settings, and categories;
  • application preferences and notification or alarm choices;
  • a one-way cryptographic representation of an optional application-lock PIN; and
  • audio recordings created or selected by you.

Android system backup is disabled for the App. We do not operate a server that receives this local content, and this content is not included in advertising or analytics events.

2. Device permissions and capabilities

  • Microphone: Used when you choose to create a voice note or use supported on-device speech features.
  • Notifications and vibration: Used to deliver reminders created by you.
  • Exact alarms, full-screen alarms, foreground media playback, wake lock, and restart handling: Used to trigger reminders at the requested time, display a full-screen alarm when selected, continue the alarm sound until dismissed, and restore future alarms after the device restarts.
  • Internet access: Used by Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and RevenueCat.
  • Advertising ID: May be used by Google AdMob in the free version for advertising, measurement, frequency control, and fraud prevention, subject to applicable consent choices and device settings.

The App does not request permission to access precise or approximate device location, contacts, camera, photos, videos, or shared device storage. Third-party services may infer an approximate location, such as a city or region, from an IP address.

3. Voice and audio processing

Voice recordings used by Hatırla remain in the App's local storage. When supported, speech processing uses Android device capabilities. The App does not upload note audio to a speech-recognition server operated by us. Availability and behavior of system speech features may depend on the device manufacturer and Android configuration.

4. Encrypted backup and restore

If you choose to create a backup, the backup is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from the password you provide. The backup is transferred only to a destination you select through the Android file or sharing interface. We do not receive or store the backup file or password. If the password is lost, we cannot recover the backup.

5. Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics is enabled by default in current Android versions of Hatırla and does not currently have an in-app opt-out control. It may process:

  • application interactions, screens viewed, feature usage, and session measurements;
  • application version, device model, operating system version, language, and region;
  • diagnostic and performance information;
  • an approximate location derived from a masked or truncated IP address; and
  • a randomly generated app-instance or similar device identifier.

Hatırla may record events such as creating an item, searching, or changing a theme. Analytics events do not contain note text, reminder text, search queries, PINs, backup passwords, or audio content. Firebase Analytics advertising-ID collection and ad-personalization signals are disabled in the App configuration. This setting is separate from Google AdMob's advertising and consent processing.

6. Advertising with Google AdMob

The free version of Hatırla uses Google AdMob to display adaptive banner advertisements and non-rewarded interstitial advertisements at natural transition points after eligible save actions. AdMob may process:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • advertising ID, app-instance identifiers, and other device identifiers;
  • device, operating system, language, and application information;
  • advertisement requests, impressions, interactions, and diagnostics; and
  • fraud-prevention and security signals.

Where required, Hatırla uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and store advertising consent choices before requesting ads. Depending on your choice and location, Google may serve personalized, non-personalized, or limited ads. You can also manage advertising choices through Android and Google account settings. Users with an active Hatırla Pro entitlement are not shown AdMob advertisements by the App.

7. Purchases with RevenueCat

Hatırla uses RevenueCat to manage and restore the optional Pro purchase. RevenueCat and the relevant app store may process an app-user identifier, purchase status, product information, transaction information, device or application information, and related diagnostics. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

9. Purposes and legal bases

We process local information as necessary to provide the features requested by you. Third-party analytics is used for our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the App, subject to applicable law. Advertising identifiers and personalized advertising are processed based on consent where consent is legally required. Non-personalized or limited advertising, security, fraud prevention, and purchase verification may rely on other lawful bases available under applicable law.

10. Retention and deletion

Local content remains on your device until you delete individual items, use the "Delete all local data" function, or uninstall the App. Removing local data also cancels associated reminders where supported. Information previously processed by Firebase Analytics, AdMob, RevenueCat, or an app store may be retained according to each provider's retention rules and legal obligations. Deleting local App data does not automatically delete records held independently by these providers.

11. Your choices and rights

You can manage microphone, notification, exact-alarm, and full-screen-alarm permissions in Android settings. Where required, the Google UMP consent form provides choices concerning advertising personalization and participating advertising partners. You may also reset or limit the advertising ID through Android settings.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection concerning personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that occurred before withdrawal. Requests concerning data controlled directly by Google, RevenueCat, or an app store may need to be submitted to that provider.

12. Children

Hatırla is not directed to children under 13, or the higher minimum age required in a user's country. We do not knowingly collect children's note content. The App configures Google Mobile Ads as not child-directed and limits maximum ad content rating to parental guidance. If you believe a child has provided personal information in violation of applicable law, contact us.

13. Security

We use measures including private application storage, disabled Android system backup, one-way PIN hashing, and password-based encryption for exported backups. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

14. International data processing

Google, Firebase, RevenueCat, and app-store providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their processing is governed by their respective privacy terms and any legally required transfer safeguards.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when App features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The current version will be identified by the "Last updated" date shown at the top of this page.
